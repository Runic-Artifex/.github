# Code of conduct

Runic Artifex is committed to a constructive, welcoming, and technically honest
community. Treat other participants with respect, assume good intent while
remaining clear about evidence, and criticize ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately to the security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`.
Maintainers will review reports discreetly and act proportionately.
